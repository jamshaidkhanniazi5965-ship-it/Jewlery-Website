Our Foundation

A legacy of excellence. A future of opportunity.

For more than four decades, Michel Germani has dedicated his life to the world of fine jewellery, from his studies at the Gemological Institute of America in Los Angeles to his professional experience in Antwerp, Switzerland and Saudi Arabia, and the establishment of Germani Jewellery in Australia in 1985.

Today, Michel Germani brings this wealth of experience to the Germani Jewellery Academy, founded on a simple belief: knowledge gained through a lifetime of practical experience should be shared with the next generation.

Our Academy opens the door to this knowledge, teaching aspiring entrepreneurs not only how to appreciate jewellery, but how to establish, manage and grow their own jewellery retail businesses with confidence, skill and vision.

Craftsmanship & Excellence

Where true luxury begins

At Germani Jewellery Academy, true luxury begins with imagination, technical mastery and an uncompromising attention to detail. Our students are introduced to the principles of fine design, gemstone presentation and precision craftsmanship that transform an idea into a timeless jewel.

From elegant proportions and refined settings to the harmony of colour, brilliance and wearability, every creation reflects the discipline, artistry and excellence at the heart of the Germani tradition.

Celebrating design skill, precision craftsmanship and timeless beauty.
What the Academy covers

  1. Appreciating Jewellery

  2. Fine Design
