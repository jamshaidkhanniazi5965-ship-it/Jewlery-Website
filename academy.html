More Than Another Qualification

Learn the knowledge behind a real jewellery business.

The Germani Jewellery Academy has been developed from 50 years of hands-on international jewellery experience. It brings together the practical knowledge, commercial understanding and judgement required to understand how a jewellery business actually works.

Knowledge Once Kept Within the Family

Traditionally, this kind of knowledge has been passed from parents to children through generations of established family businesses. The skills, experience, commercial understanding and closely guarded secrets of the jewellery trade were often kept within the family and handed from one generation to the next.

The Academy opens that practical knowledge to a new generation of students who want to build their own future in jewellery.

No University Degree Required

You do not need a university degree to attend the Academy. This is not about collecting qualifications to hang on a wall. It is about gaining practical knowledge, real-world skills and the confidence to take greater control of your own career, make informed choices and work towards the future and lifestyle you want to create.

Learn first-hand what it takes to become a jewellery store owner and operator - practical knowledge that cannot be gained through theory alone.

Academy Overview

A Practical Education in Jewellery Retail

The Germani Jewellery Academy has been developed from 50 years of hands-on experience across the international jewellery industry. The programme brings together practical retail knowledge, commercial understanding and the professional skills required to build and operate a successful jewellery business.

More Than Jewellery Knowledge

Traditional education can provide theory. Successful retail requires the ability to apply knowledge in the real world. This programme connects the jewellery product with the commercial disciplines behind a successful retail operation: buying, pricing, merchandising, sales, customer service, marketing, staffing and business management.

Business Ownership Mindset

A successful owner does not need to perform every task personally. Students are introduced to the importance of systems, procedures, delegation and specialist support - including valuers, repair specialists, manufacturers, gemstone and diamond specialists, marketing professionals and recruitment services.

The long-term objective is to build a business that can operate professionally, serve customers consistently and create opportunities for sustainable growth.

Business & Brand

Your Business. Your Brand. Your Future.

The principles taught by the Academy can support a range of business models, whether you plan to operate under your own name, develop a private brand, build an online jewellery business or work with an established brand or licence.

Course Programme

The Six Course Areas

01 - Jewellery Knowledge

Weeks 1-4

Build a strong foundation in jewellery products, the industry, valuation, retail presentation and essential business fundamentals.

Industry & Product Knowledge

  • Design, manufacturing, wholesale, distribution and retail
  • Silver, gold and fashion jewellery
  • Cubic zirconia and stainless-steel jewellery
  • Diamonds, gemstones, opals, pearls, beads and carvings
  • Engagement rings, vintage and antique jewellery
  • Related luxury and fashion products

Valuation & Pricing

  • Gold, diamond and gemstone pricing
  • Jewellery pricing and margins
  • Wholesale and retail pricing
  • Insurance and independent valuations
  • Diamond and gemstone certificates
  • Auction prices and market values

Retail Confidence

  • Product knowledge and confident presentation
  • Client relationships and follow-up
  • Merchandising, packaging and display
  • Advertising, promotion, catalogues and social media
  • Guarantees, returns, cleaning and after-sales services

Business Fundamentals

  • Stock lists and inventory
  • GST, tax and refunds
  • Profit and loss
  • Wages and director’s loan accounts
  • Accounting systems and software

02 - Retail Operations

Weeks 5-7

Explore what it takes to establish, operate and grow a jewellery retail business - from location and fit-out through to people, marketing and financial management.

Establishing the Store

  • Selecting a location and obtaining a lease
  • Shop design, fittings and fit-out
  • Local and overseas stock sourcing
  • Purchasing, importing and pricing merchandise
  • Inventory and accounting systems
  • Understanding margins and overheads
  • Recruiting and developing the right team

Marketing & Launch

  • Advertising and promotion
  • Store launches and events
  • Brand ambassadors
  • Creating your own brand
  • Complementary product lines: watches, perfume, fashion accessories, handbags and leather goods
  • Selling other brands and operating under licence

Business & Financial Management

  • Profit and loss, GST and BAS
  • Insurance and security
  • Wages and financial management
  • Business planning and financial projections
  • Comparing jewellery and luxury retail business models
  • Understanding overheads, margins and profitability

Expansion

  • Explore opportunities to develop additional stores and extend operations locally and overseas.

03 - Manufacturing

Weeks 8-10

Understand the journey from jewellery concept to finished product, and explore the foundations of a workshop or vertically integrated operation.

Design & Production

  • Jewellery design and CAD
  • 3D printing and casting
  • Handmade jewellery
  • Polishing and rhodium finishing
  • Gold chains and gold stamping
  • Solid versus hollow construction
  • Local and overseas manufacturing

Workshop Management

  • Employing experienced jewellers and trainees
  • Employment versus subcontracting
  • Purchasing manufacturing equipment
  • New and second-hand equipment
  • Establishing or acquiring a workshop or factory
  • Using trade fairs to source equipment and opportunities

Commercial Manufacturing

  • Supply your own stores
  • Wholesale to other retailers
  • Local and overseas trade fairs
  • Import and export
  • Government export grants
  • Develop an A-to-Z vertically integrated operation

Tools & Equipment Covered

  • 10x loupe and microscope
  • Tweezers and gold testing equipment
  • Master stones
  • Specific gravity testing unit
  • Fluorescence unit and spectroscope
  • Gold and gemstone scales
  • Diamond tester

04 - Buying & Selling

Weeks 11-12

Develop practical approaches to sourcing jewellery and building confident, relationship-based sales.

Buying & Sourcing

  • Manufacture in-house or subcontract
  • Buy from local and overseas wholesalers
  • Source from factories and trade fairs
  • Purchase through auctions and online platforms
  • Buy on credit or consignment
  • Import goods and understand duties and taxes
  • Purchase semi-finished products
  • Understand supplier and return policies

Selling with Confidence

  • Product knowledge and self-confidence
  • Self-awareness and communication
  • Understanding different customer personalities
  • Building long-term client relationships
  • Customer follow-up and VIP engagement
  • Free cleaning, repairs and valuations
  • Social media product launches
  • Influencers, models and brand ambassadors

The Client Experience: combine strong product knowledge with professional presentation, packaging and personalised service to create lasting client relationships.

05 - Gemmology

Weeks 13-14

Develop practical knowledge of diamonds and gemstones, including identification, grading, buying and sourcing.

Diamonds

  • The Four Cs: Colour, Clarity, Carat and Cut
  • Brilliant and old-cut diamonds
  • Diamond grading and buying
  • Natural and laboratory-grown diamonds
  • Natural fancy-colour and pink diamonds
  • Local and international sourcing
  • Wholesalers, trade organisations and auction houses
  • Online diamond purchasing

Gemstone Identification & Buying

  • Ruby, sapphire and emerald
  • Aquamarine and tourmaline
  • Garnet, amethyst and peridot
  • Onyx, lapis lazuli and jade
  • Pearls and opals
  • Beads and carvings
  • High-end and semi-precious gemstones
  • Synthetic stones

Jewellery & Gemstone Sourcing

  • Locally and internationally manufactured jewellery
  • Buying from second-hand sources
  • Online sourcing
  • Guarantees and return policies
  • Exploring gemstones as potential investments

06 - Branding & Awareness

Weeks 15-16

Build a distinctive brand, understand modern marketing channels and develop the leadership awareness required to grow a jewellery business.

Build the Brand

  • Create and develop your own brand
  • Sell under licence
  • Offer complementary brands
  • Build an integrated jewellery and luxury business
  • Brand identity and market position
  • Packaging and presentation
  • Store location, design and fit-out
  • Department stores, shopping centres, street-front and shop-in-shop opportunities

Marketing & Growth

  • Website and social media
  • Digital and promotional platforms
  • Media and television advertising
  • Outdoor advertising and signage
  • Events, launches and VIP activities
  • Celebrity and brand ambassadors
  • Sponsorship, charity and community involvement

Self-Awareness & Business Leadership

  • Personal motivation and continuous improvement
  • Understanding different personality categories
  • Building confidence and business awareness
  • Ongoing knowledge and training
  • Attracting the right staff and partners
  • Working on the business - not only in the business
  • Leadership, communication and community
Course Outcome

Course Outcome

By completing the programme, students will develop a broad understanding of the jewellery business - from product knowledge and gemmology through to sourcing, manufacturing, retailing, marketing, branding and business management.

The aim is to provide the knowledge and confidence to work within the jewellery industry, establish a jewellery business, or develop an existing operation into a stronger and more integrated business.

Knowledge

Understand products, processes and the wider industry.

Confidence

Make informed retail and commercial decisions.

Commercial Awareness

Connect jewellery expertise with sound business management.

A practical foundation for the next generation of jewellery retail professionals and business owners.
