Made for one person

An engagement ring, an anniversary gift or a piece to mark a lifetime: designed with you by Michel Germani and made by hand.

Engagement & Bridal

The ring of a lifetime

Custom engagement rings are at the heart of the Germani house. Choose a diamond with Michel’s guidance, whether a classic round brilliant, an elegant pear or a rare coloured stone, then shape the setting and band around it.

Matching wedding bands can be designed alongside, so that the pair sits perfectly together.

The Process

From first conversation to final piece

  1. Consultation

    Meet Michel privately to talk through ideas, budget and timing. Bring inspiration, or simply a feeling.

  2. The Stone

    View a curated selection of diamonds and coloured gemstones, and learn what makes each one special.

  3. Design

    Michel prepares sketches and detailed designs, refined together until every proportion feels right.

  4. Craftsmanship

    Your piece is handcrafted, carefully set and finished, then presented to you in person.

Coloured Gemstones

Rare colour, set with care

Sapphires, emeralds, rubies and Australian black opals each call for their own approach. Calibré-cut frames, diamond halos and hand-finished mounts are designed to bring out the colour of the centre stone.

Michel can also redesign heirloom jewellery, giving treasured stones a new life.
