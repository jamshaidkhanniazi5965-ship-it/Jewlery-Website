I · History & Foundation

Michel Germani

From his studies at the Gemological Institute of America in Los Angeles, where he qualified as a Graduate Gemologist (G.G., G.I.A.), to professional experience in Antwerp, Switzerland and Saudi Arabia, Michel Germani established Germani Jewellery in Australia in 1985.

Since then he has established and operated more than 20 stores in Sydney, including boutiques at the Queen Victoria Building and the Hilton Sydney, and built one of Sydney’s most recognised jewellery names.

Germani’s special commissions include a necklace made for a Queen in 1984, an emerald parure made for a Saudi Princess in 1983, and a collection made for Elizabeth Taylor with the launch of her perfumes.
