Bespoke

Your piece, from first sketch

Each commission is a one-to-one collaboration with Michel Germani.

  1. Consultation

    A private meeting to understand the story, the wearer and the occasion.

  2. The Stone

    A selection of exceptional diamonds and coloured gemstones to choose from.

  3. Design

    Sketches and renderings, refined together until every detail is right.

  4. Craftsmanship

    The piece is made by hand, then finished and presented to you.
